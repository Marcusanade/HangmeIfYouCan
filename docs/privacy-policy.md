# Privacy Policy — HangmeIfYouCan

_Last updated: October 2026_

## Overview

HangmeIfYouCan is a word-guessing game developed by byMarcusss. This policy explains how the app handles data.

## Data Collection

**HangmeIfYouCan does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.**

The app does not:
- Require an account or registration
- Access the internet
- Use analytics or advertising SDKs
- Access your contacts, camera, microphone, or location

## Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores the following data **locally on your device only**, using Android's standard SharedPreferences mechanism:

- Game statistics (games played, wins, streaks)
- Multiplayer player names entered by the user
- Sound mute preference

This data never leaves your device and can be deleted at any time by uninstalling the app or using the in-app "Reset statistics" option.

## Third-Party Services

The app uses no third-party services, SDKs, or APIs.

## Children

This app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.
